About Yitai Food

Yitai Food was founded in 1995 in China's Yangtze River Delta region. Since 2004, Yitai has operated together with the former state-owned Changzhou Dongfeng Candy and Cold Food Factory, whose brand heritage dates to 1958.

Over nearly three decades, Yitai has continued to build R&D, production and service capabilities around plant-based extract beverages, instant beverages, concentrates, ready-to-drink beverages and oat nutrition foods.

The company values real ingredients and stable production, carries forward the Changzhou flavor memory of osmanthus sour plum crystal, and serves household consumption, foodservice, commercial beverages, OEM/ODM and overseas markets.
