Yitai Food Manufacturing Background

Food manufacturing experience traceable to 1958.

Yitai Food was established in 1995 and continues manufacturing capabilities with roots traceable to 1958.

Its physical factory, production lines and product development team support powdered beverages, concentrates, ready-to-drink beverages, oat products and commercial programs. Quality systems include FSSC 22000 and HACCP.
