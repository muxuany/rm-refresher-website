About Yitai Food

Philosophy & Responsibility

Focused on plant-based beverages, milk tea products, and oat nutrition

Yitai Food does not pursue a broad food portfolio. It continues to build capabilities around plant-based beverages and oat nutrition, with long-term stability rather than unstable rapid growth as a guiding principle.

Corporate Responsibility
