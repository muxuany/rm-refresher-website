About Yitai Food

Raw Material Bases & Research

From raw material foundation to product development

From ingredient bases to university cooperation, Yitai strengthens product research, process optimization and transformation capabilities.

Planting Base

Raw Material Base Development

Yitai Food develops raw material bases for figs, black rice, purple sweet potato, osmanthus and other crops. Its company + base + standardization approach supports cultivation, processing and traceability.

Base development strengthens raw material consistency at the source for plant-based beverages, grain foods and future product development.

Research Cooperation

University & Research Cooperation

Yitai Food works with universities and research institutions on food research, technical exchange and project development, including the University of Science and Technology of China Suzhou Institute, Jiangnan University, Jiangsu Academy of Agricultural Sciences and Changzhou University.

Its work with Changzhou University includes experimental and internship bases that support R&D, testing and product commercialization.
